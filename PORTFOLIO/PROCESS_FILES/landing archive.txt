Kylie Bi

Communication Designer & Creative Producer

Short Positioning

I explore unseen possibilities and translate complex ideas into coherent communication experiences through research, narrative systems, visual communication, and creative production.

Introduction

Every project begins with a question.

Rather than starting with a medium, I begin by understanding the relationships behind a problem—its context, the people involved, and the conditions that shape communication.

My practice moves between research, concept development, visual systems, and production, translating abstract ideas into experiences that people can understand, engage with, and participate in.

Working across speculative design, editorial systems, exhibition narratives, and integrated brand communication, I see design not as the creation of objects, but as the construction of communication.

Closing Statement

Design begins before form.

The medium follows the problem.

Communication is an act of translation.


你很多项目真正设计的不是最后那个 artefact，而是让某种交流、理解或意义能够自然发生的语境（context）。这个思路与 Brand DNA 中「The problem determines the medium」「Communication is an act of translation」以及 Creative Brief 中「Thinking always precedes form」形成了非常一致的逻辑

Archive

Intro

This archive is not organised by discipline.

Each project represents a different response to a different communication question.

Together they document an evolving design practice—one that moves across visual communication, editorial design, speculative systems, exhibitions, photography, and brand experiences while remaining grounded in the same methodology.

Rather than presenting isolated outcomes, the archive reveals how questions become contexts, how contexts become communication systems, and how ideas become experiences.
